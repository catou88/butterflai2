## Definitions and limits

“Conditional” matters: held-out wings supply alignment information and retrospective full-cycle area. These are not prospective solar-cycle forecasts. The top raw and physics-aligned boosting means differ by less than their paired cycle SE; do not claim a unique winning algorithm. The best likelihood model is not the best total-count model.

The synthetic L1 error is a single controlled experiment, not real-world magnetic-field accuracy. Numerical derivative agreement is a code-validation metric, not predictive accuracy. The three sealed cycles were not scored. Feature importance and a well-fitting intensity do not establish a causal buoyancy mechanism or measure internal field strength. No deep neural network or production deployment is claimed.
